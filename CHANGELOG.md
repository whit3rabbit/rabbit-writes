# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (10 August 2026)

First release.

- `human-writing` engine: 63-pattern catalog in P0/P1/P2 tiers, false-positive
  discipline, six register profiles with a tolerance matrix, Orwell and
  ASD-STE100 craft layer, 32-item behaviorally anchored self-check.
- `scan.py`: fingerprint detection (hidden unicode, AI tracking parameters,
  chat citation leaks, unfilled placeholders), tiered vocabulary with density
  and cluster rules, stylometrics (burstiness, MATTR, trigram repetition,
  sentence and paragraph variation), self-reference exemption, reliability
  gating by word count. Reports named findings and deliberately emits no single
  AI score.
- `verify.py`: preservation validator for rewrites.
- `rabbit-writes`: voice-aware drafting skill with a pluggable voice slot.
- `voice-setup`: interview, sample measurement, editing, and blending for voice
  profiles.
- Voice band in `scan.py` via `--voice-rules`, never relaxed by a register
  profile.
